How it works /

  • Nothing extra to install

    RigSync is a lightweight Windows app built in Python, with nothing extra to install.

  • No middleman

    It uses Git under the hood to sync your files straight between your computers and your own GitHub account.

  • Your login stays safe

    Your login stays in your computer's built-in password vault.
